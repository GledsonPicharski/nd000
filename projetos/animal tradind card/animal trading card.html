Small Dog

A dog's nose print is unique, much like a human fingerprint, and its sense of smell is tens of thousands of times more sensitive than ours.

  • Scientific Name: Canis lupus familiaris
  • Average Weight: Under 10 kilograms
  • Average Lifespan: 12 to 16 years
  • Habitat: Homes all over the world

Small dogs are loyal, playful and full of energy, making them some of the most popular companions around the globe. Their compact size makes them great for apartments, and despite being little, many of them are brave and alert watchdogs who love to be close to their families.
